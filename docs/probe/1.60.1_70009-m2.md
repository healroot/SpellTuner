What the dumps show (planner's reading):
- **Attribution mixes a HoT with a direct heal.** Rejuvenation R2 (48 over 12 s = four ticks of 12
  every 3 s) is credited `12+12+110` "every 1.9 s": the 110 is a Healing Touch landing. The Healing
  Touch after it is credited `12` -- a Rejuvenation tick -- and marked BELOW range. The first Healing
  Touch "nothing landed" is the one whose 110 went to the HoT. `UNIT_COMBAT` carries no spell id, so
  a direct heal must be paired by time with its own `UNIT_SPELLCAST_SUCCEEDED`, and a HoT's ticks by
  their cadence from its application; a BELOW verdict built on a mis-paired amount is the false Q10
  signal the measure exists to avoid.
- **Damage over time is never captured:** Moonfire R2's periodic part reads "0 ticks" twice, and
  Rejuvenation R2 once in combat.
- **Wrath R1 `landed 6`** against 15-18: a partial resist (vanilla resists in quarters) or a
  mis-pairing -- the measure cannot tell which and should not call it BELOW without saying so.
- **In combat `+heal ?`**: the bonus-healing read is secret or unavailable in combat.
- Healing Touch R2 `landed 115` (the top of 90-115) is the one clean direct-heal sample; whether it
  landed on a full-health target is not recorded, so the gross-or-effective question is still open.
