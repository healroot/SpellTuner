What the eighth report settles (2026-09-28):

- **Q3 answered for a party member: `UNIT_COMBAT` on `party1` is plain in combat** -- 5 WOUND and 4
  HEAL, every one readable. With the seventh report, the event the recorder rests on survives
  combat on every unit a healer tracks.
- **Q2 answered, and it is the hard one: a party member's health is secret -- current AND max.**
  `UnitHealth(party1)` and `UnitHealthMax(party1)` are secret in combat and out of it, with the
  party member present. The player's own max is plain (252), a party member's is not. So a party
  member's health cannot be reconstructed as a fraction from `UNIT_COMBAT` alone: the recorder can
  track the **deficit** (WOUND adds, HEAL subtracts, never below zero, anchored at zero out of
  combat), which is the number a heal decision needs, but not the percentage or the danger line
  without a max. Not asked yet: `C_Secrets.ShouldUnitHealthMaxBeSecret("party1")` -- the probe only
  asks it for `player`.
- **Q5 answered: party auras are readable out of combat and raise in combat.** Out of combat
  `party1`'s first buff reads whole (Mark of the Wild, from the player, 3600 s); in combat the same
  "Auras cannot be accessed when secret while tainted" error as on the player. Pre-pull HoTs come
  from the last out-of-combat read, as the plan already assumed.
- **Q1: a level-up with no gear change moved a HEAL.** Healing Touch R2 went "88 to 112" -> "89 to
  114" at level 8 -> 9, bonus healing 0 both times; R1 (40-55) and Rejuvenation R1 (32) did not
  move. So the heal texts are dynamic too, and a rank's base value grows with the caster's level
  (vanilla's per-level increase, which R1 has presumably exhausted -- inference). The four damage
  texts went *down* because bonus damage went 5 -> 0 (the elixir wore off): that is the second
  proof of the spell-power term, and the per-point slope is readable from it (Wrath R1 17-20 ->
  15-18, i.e. +5 spell damage bought +2 at both ends).
