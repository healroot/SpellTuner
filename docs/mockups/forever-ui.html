SpellTuner on Forever: UI mockups

These mockups go with SPEC-forever-ui.md and are drawn at 1:1 in UI units, using the spec's palette and sizes. The numbers are your level 10 druid's, from the screenshots. Web fonts stand in for the game's: Friz Quadrata where installed (else Georgia), and Arial Narrow for numbers. The blurred backdrop stands in for the game world. The window is 96% opaque, so the world barely shows through. Each window's 20 px header hangs above its frame, as the kit draws it: the 860 x 560 frame is 579 px tall with its header.

1Main window: Spells, Healing Touch open

The rail lists your spells and each row is one view. The decision strip says which rank and how it compares, without repeating the table. In the table, the suggested rank has a 2 px accent bar, a faint fill and the best tag. There is no gold. Numbers are right-aligned in Arial Narrow, and the per-mana bar shows the trade-off at a glance. The card holds what the table does not: the spell's own text, the range and the crit. Healing Touch and Rejuvenation are the seed; Moonfire and Wrath were added by hand, so no row carries the new dot (mockup 2 shows it).

2Overview, with a rail row hovered and a removal undoable

Overview puts every listed spell on one line: the suggested rank against your highest. Export lives here. Whole book is today's Spellbook table under the new look: a header row per family with + to add, and every rank under it in the RANKS columns. Hovering Moonfire in the rail swaps its rank for move up, move down and remove. After a removal, the rail offers Undo until your next change. On the right, the rail as it will look at level 12: Regrowth, a heal the reconcile appended on its own, carries the new dot until it is opened.

3List editor: the picker sheet, while Wrath is dragged above Moonfire

The picker is a sheet inside the main window, never another window. Its mask covers the spell view but not the rail, so the rail stays live: you can drop a spell from your spellbook onto it, or reorder, while the picker is open. Ticking a spell adds it and unticking removes it. The rail shows a drag in progress: the dragged row is faded and a 2 px accent line marks the drop point.

4Tooltip block, under the game's own lines

The block starts after a blank line, with an accent header, grey labels and white values. It never repeats the game's range or cast line. The plain block is the header plus 4 lines: the suggestion comes first, and "~N now" (in mana blue) appears only when the modelled pool is below its max (~290 of 364 here). "Shift" in dark grey is the only hint. A spell with no heal or damage in its text (Mark of the Wild) gets only the pool line and no hint; a free one gets no block. A description read before combat would add one red line, last in the plain block.

5Window arrangement (a 1923 x 1080 UI space, drawn at 23%)

Two big windows are never on screen together. The main window is in HIGH strata; the replay and practice windows are in DIALOG, marked by the dashed outline. A replay or a practice session takes the main window's place and offers the way back (decision 3). Review and Practice are 1036 wide, so with the main window centred about 443 units are free on each side, less than even a one-column replay: docking beside it where it stands would almost never fit. The fourth panel is the alternative, pair mode, which is specified but not scheduled. The clock (bottom right) is a HUD and is never moved.
