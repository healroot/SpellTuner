SpellTuner mana clock, version 2: what it says, how big it is, mana and the five-second rule together (C1-C6)

A design for approval before anything is built. It answers the author's feedback on 0.16.6 (below) and changes the clock that 0.16.6 shipped (T93, T98, T102: UI/ClockView.lua, UI/ClockSettings.lua, Engine/ClockFace.lua), in the conventions of docs/mockups/next.html M8. Every clock is drawn at its real pixel size; the rows marked 2x are the same pixels doubled. Flat is the style throughout; one row is Ellesmere (C3). All text inside a mocked frame is ASCII, as it will be in game.

The author, on 0.16.6: "the height does not really change anything / I would like to have different text options on what to show and customizable size of the clock / Also 5-sec rule bar - I like it but the fillment should be more smooth / Show time to oom as a bar is not informative / Also I wouild like to make both mana and 5 sec rule to be visible (we can make it as separate bars or as a single bar with some animation indicator for 5s rule)"

The two lines. On TBC every number is read plain: mana, the five-second rule (MD.Regen:FSRRemaining), regen. On Forever the current mana is secret: the clock's times are the model's (~, rounded to 5 s, never an arrow), the real mana bar is drawn by the game (MD.API.DrawUnitPower: the secret goes straight into a status bar and is never read), and the five-second rule is the model's, from your last priced own cast (MD.Pool.model.lastSpend). A real mana number or percent as text on Forever waits for probe Q-clock-2 (can a secret be set into a font string?); until then those options show the model's ~ value. Marks: ~ Forever modelled on Forever, after the probe waits for Q-clock-2, TBC only, new not in 0.16.6. Times, mana and percentages in the clocks are illustrative.

Not in this file: the two bugs in the same message (the simulator animating a GCD after a direct cast lands; Overview -> Whole book empty until scrolled) are fixes, not designs, and go to their own tasks.

C3Mana and the five-second rule visible together: three designs on each layout, three moments

spec 7.2 bar sourcesdecision 15 bar defaultnew task after approvalfills illustrative

Each block shows one design on Line, Compact and Bar at three moments: mid-rule (cast 2 s ago, 3.0 s of the rule left, mana 62 %), after the rule (spirit regen running, mana 71 % and rising) and out of mana soon (just cast, 9 %, 15 s). The Forever constraint: the mana fill is drawn by the game from a secret value and SpellTuner never learns where it ends, so on Forever nothing may sit at the fill's edge; every 5SR mark below is placed by time alone (seconds since your last priced cast) and is drawn the same over any fill, which is why all three work on both lines. The mana bar's colour is ours to set on both lines; its length is the game's on Forever.

C6The smooth 5SR fill

spec 7.2 sparknew task after approvalframe times illustrative

The 5SR fill is computed every frame from the time of your last spend ((now - lastSpend) / 5, one SetValue in the clock's own OnUpdate, no allocation) instead of on Forever's 0.5-s tick (UI/Clock_Forever.lua paints on MD:OnTick: 16-px jumps on a 160-px bar) or TBC's 0.1-s throttle (UI/Widget.lua: 3.2-px jumps), while the words keep their 0.25 / 0.5 s cadence. The same frame-by-frame value drives the veil (B); the chip (C) is animated by the client itself. A new cast still restarts the strip at 0 at once: that jump is the rule, not a stutter. QUESTIONS FOR THE AUTHOR
1. Mana and the 5SR: A, two bars (recommended), B one bar with the veil, or C the swipe chip? Keep B as the Join: One bar option? (C3)
2. One default on both lines, Mana + 5SR, replacing decision 15 (a) (5SR on TBC, pool on Forever)? This makes Line 2 px taller (180 x 32) and gives Compact bars it did not have. (C2, C4)
