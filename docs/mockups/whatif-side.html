M2What if as a drawer on the window's right edge

The view is exactly today's closed layout: RANKS keeps its 540 px and every column. WHAT IF opens as a 220 x 560 drawer flush with the window's right edge, outside it, a child of the main frame (so it moves, scales, hides in combat and on TakeOver with the window). Its contents are the same vertical layout as M1 at 204 px. If the window sits closer than 220 px to the right screen edge, the drawer opens on the left edge instead.

220 px, outside
  • Width: RANKS untouched at 540 / 524 px of columns at every offset; the drawer's 204 px of content is 4 px more than M1's column. Nothing in the table is abbreviated.
  • Height: the drawer has the full 560 (536 under its title bar), so +2 fits without a scroll (about 438 px used).
  • TBC strip: unchanged at 164 px. Optional: move the toggle to an edge tab on the window and give the comparison the button's 72 px back (236 px).
  • MD.Win and ESC: no size change if it overlays outside; the drawer pushes onto MD.EscStack when it opens and is removed when it closes, so the first ESC closes the drawer and the second the window. Combat hide and TakeOver need nothing (child frame). New: the screen-edge flip, and the drawer is not covered by the window's SetClampedToScreen. Widening the window instead (MD.Win resize to 1080 per toggle) is possible but fights SIZES.minW and the saved position; not proposed.
  • Effort: small to medium. One new BackdropTemplate frame, PaintWhatIf's vertical layout (shared with M1 if both are ever wanted), Push / Remove on the ESC stack, the edge flip. LayoutBlocks only loses the WHAT IF block; the tables and card are untouched.

CClosed state, and how each opens

Both options open from today's toggle, What if... at the strip's right (SpellsPane:ToggleWhatIf, open state per session). Closed, the view is today's closed view in both: RANKS at 540, 13 rows to y 425, the card at 437. M1 must switch the table between its wide and narrow column sets on every toggle (or keep the narrow set always, wasting 200 px when closed). M2 only shows or hides the drawer; the button reads What if > closed and What if < open, and an optional 16-px edge tab (drawn) opens it too.

RRecommendation

M2, the drawer. It answers the ask completely - every rank visible, at full width, while the stats change - and it is the only option with width to spare at +2. M1 fits, but only by taking Lvl, the tag words and most of the Per mana bar out of RANKS exactly while you are comparing ranks, and its column is already over at +2. M2 costs a frame outside the window (1080 wide while open), an ESC stack entry and a left-edge fallback near the screen edge; none of that touches MD.Win's sizes. Choose M1 instead only if nothing may ever be drawn outside the window.
