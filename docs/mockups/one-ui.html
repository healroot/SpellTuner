One Spells UI on both lines (0.17): mockups for approval

For docs/SPEC-one-ui.md. You asked for a shared pane, Forever's features on TBC and TBC's (What if) on Forever, plus TBC's other classes, TBC damage spells, Settings and window sizes, and the tooltip words. Every frame is drawn at 1:1 in UI units with the Flat style (druid accent). TBC numbers are the model's own (Engine/RankMath.lua under the offline harness: the stub druid, level 64, +700 healing, 6500 mana; the What if adds 150 healing). Forever numbers are the level 11 druid from the earlier mockups. Anything marked ILLUSTRATIVE was not computed: no damage values have been verified, so the damage and priest numbers are placeholders. All in-game text is ASCII.
