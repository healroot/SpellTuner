Rank marks: best, max and eff instead of one "best" (mockup for approval)

For the author's design of 2026-10-04. Spells/RankRules.lua gives each family three marks instead of RR.Suggested; UI/SpellsPane.lua draws one tag per row (best > max > eff), the decision strip compares best with max, the rank card names every mark the rank holds with one line saying why. Every frame is 1:1 in UI units in the Flat style (druid accent), the RANKS table at its real columns (Rank 52, Lvl 34, Mana 46, value 58, Per mana 116 with a 72 px bar, Per sec 54, Cast 46, Casts 58, tag 60 starting at x = 480). Healing Touch at +700 uses the model's own numbers from docs/mockups/one-ui.html M1; everything marked ILLUSTRATIVE was made up to show the rule, and the marks on this page were computed from the numbers shown by the rule below, not placed by hand. All in-game text is ASCII.

M2Forever: a rank at x0.6 damage and x3 efficiency ILLUSTRATIVE NUMBERS

A level 24 druid; Rank 4 costs far more than Rank 3 for 1.67 times the damage (the kind of step a retail-engine cost table may produce). Rank 3: 3.38 per mana vs 1.11 (x3.04), 60% of the damage per second. Score at k = 1: (3.04 - 1) - (1 - 0.60) = +1.64, so best = Rank 3, which is also eff (one tag, best; the card lists both). Rank 4 is max in white. Today's rule would also pick Rank 3 (60% is over the 40% floor), but at 35% of the damage it would silently fall back to Rank 4; the new rule keeps choosing Rank 3 while the trade pays.

?Questions (first option recommended)

  • Q1. Is best chosen only among non-dominated ranks? The rule as written scores every rank; a dominated rank can score positive only if it beats max per mana, and then the eff rank beats it on both. Recommended: score the non-dominated ranks only (this page does), so a best is never also beaten.
  • Q2. Do marks follow After overheal? Today the Pareto filter and the suggestion stay on raw numbers (Engine/RankMath.lua: "never instead of them"). Recommended: marks stay raw and the card says so; the right half of M4 is the alternative, where ticking After overheal moves them.
  • Q3. Lifebloom. With equal cost and the GCD as the interval, its rows can never split the marks (M4). Recommended: accept it (the tag says which way to cast it). Alternative: price a rolled stack per second over its refresh interval, a model change outside this task.
  • Q4. "max" changes meaning. Today max is "your highest known rank"; now it is "most per second". When a lower rank has more per second, the top rank loses its tag. Recommended: accept; the card and the header line "Rank 12 of 12 known" still say which is highest.
  • Q5. Rail, Overview and My spells. They show the suggested rank today (R12 on the rail, the Suggested column). Recommended: they follow best and the column is renamed Best; not drawn here.
  • Q6. Tie at score 0 or equal marks. Recommended: a positive score is required (0 goes to max) and the first row wins ties, as RR.Suggested does today.
